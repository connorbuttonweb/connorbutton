Foreman Project Portfolio

Leadership & Construction Management • Pines Landscaping

The following are projects led by myself. For more projects I was a part of visit Pines Landscaping through their website or on Instagram. Many of these photos and videos were taken by Ruairi Duggan from Motion Filming.

134 Landry Ln – 16 Unit Condominium

The complete construction of the front and back entrances to the 16-unit neighbourhood. Planting of 36 mature trees and front gardens for all units, with the engineered grading of the whole property. Drainage piping, electrical conduit, and communication cable were run throughout the entire property prior to asphalt and grading.

The Twin Peaks | Outdoor Wet-Lay Patio

I oversaw the complete renovation of a 5500 sq ft outdoor porcelain patio at a property dubbed “The Twin Peaks”. This was compounded by consistent 40km/h winds that routinely tore down the tarped structure we had built to keep us dry and warm in the cold.

Clarks Pools - Singhampton | Pool Excavation & Patios

Contracted by Clarks Pools to complete the excavation and patios surrounding a concrete in-ground pool. In addition to this was an outdoor shower, fencing, and a sunken patio by the house. Completed in just 3 weeks.

Poplar Patios | Natural Stone Walkways & Drainage

Non-invasive project installing natural stone stepping and walkways near Osler Ski Club. We re-graded low spots of their lawn, buried their downspouts, and installed steps and walkways on the front, back, and side entrances.

Goldie Cres | Paver Driveway & Artificial Turf

The first project I was left to lead the team on my own. Oversee the creation of a full Unilock paver driveway and an artificial turf backyard with a firepit, patio, hot tub pad, and lighting.
